# Data attribution

The files in `data/` are from https://github.com/Vonter/india-aviation-traffic,
which collects data published by India's Directorate General of Civil Aviation (DGCA)
and the Ministry of Civil Aviation. The database is made available under the
Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0: http://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1.0/

If you share an adapted version of the database, you must attribute the sources
and share it under the same licence.
